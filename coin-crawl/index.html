Training

Retire

Reach floor 10 to retire. Your hero earns tales (+10% damage and gold each, forever), then you pick a class for your next hero. Gold, training, skills, allies and floor reset. Your equipment, weapon forge and enchants, ranks, class evolutions, relics, gems, runes, shards, tales, the book and achievements stay.
