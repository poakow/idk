Training parts evolve at levels 100, 250, 500 and 1000. Evolved parts reset when you retire, so each run climbs the ladder again.

Skills

Unlock by reaching floors

Skills fire on their own while Auto skills is on. Tap a skill under the dungeon, or press its number key, to fire it yourself. Hold to level up fast.

Allies

They follow you down

Hire helpers with gold. They walk behind your hero and pitch in. They go home when you retire.

Rank trials

Rookie

Beat a rank examiner to promote. Every rank is permanent: +30% damage, +30% health, +10% gold. Ranks stay when you retire.

Side dungeons

30-second runs against a huge monster. The more health bars you break (up to 25), the bigger the reward. Each costs a key. Keys refill over time, even while you're away.
