idk Games

Two small games that run in your browser. No install, no account. Your progress saves on this device.

Coin Crawl

An idle dungeon crawler. Your hero runs and fights on their own while you tap for combos, summon gear, pets and skills, evolve your class, and dive into the Endless Abyss.

  • Idle RPG
  • Tap combos
  • Desktop and phone
Play Coin Crawl

Orbit Hop

A one-button space game. Let go of your orbit at the right moment and get caught by the next planet before you drift into the dark.

  • Arcade
  • One button
  • Quick runs
